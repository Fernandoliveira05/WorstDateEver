# Bedroom model review

Reviewed the generated desktop scene from both sides of the room and at the desk and clothing cabinet. The assessment groups repeated objects with the same design. Small drawer props, exterior construction and character rig variants were also reviewed in the model and scene sources.

## Room and furniture

| Model | What identifies it | Art finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Floor, baseboards, walls, ceiling | Plank joints, timber grain, layered trim and plaster | The grain and wall finish break up the flat color without competing with props. |
| Bed frame, headboard, mattress | Distinct wood frame, legs, piped upholstery and mattress seam | The bed reads as separate materials, and the rear placement clears the doorway. |
| Blanket | Layered folds, edge bindings and woven surface | The bands distinguish it from the pillow and mattress in the wide shot. |
| Pillows | Piped edges, fabric grain and filled corners | Visible seams support the stiffer deformation. The two colors share a silhouette. |
| Nightstand | Timber top and legs, drawer seam and projecting handle | The front faces into the room. The alarm, lamp and phone fit without overhanging the top. |
| Clothing cabinet | Painted carcass, exposed edges, wood fronts and separate pulls | The wood grain resolves the front panels. The shelf makes a visible place to lay out clothes. |
| Computer desk | Wood top, painted drawer bank and clear knee space | Lowered sill and forward monitor stand keep both surfaces distinct. |
| Office chair | Back panel, separate arms, central column and five spokes | The chair has a clear silhouette. The seat and back share textured upholstery. |
| Bookcase and books | Timber frame, shelf divisions, spines and page edges | Recognizable as a group. Individual spine titles are still absent. |
| Rug | Woven surface, bands and fringe | The low profile marks the walkway without reading as a raised platform. |
| Door and frame | Inset panels, jamb, moving lever and latch plate | Hardware remains the strongest cue at arm's length. The short hallway is intentionally sparse. |
| Mirror | Timber frame, thin metal inset and a live full-length reflection | Side-wall placement gives the reflection room to show the body. |
| Window and curtains | Sill, continuous frame, mullion, rod and hanging pleats | Lowered opening puts the street at eye level. Curtain fabric holds the same palette across lighting modes. |
